## EXECUTIVE VERDICT

**Gravitea has no product to sell yet.** The 6 backend modules (auth, core, facturacion, inventario, ventas, sync) are a platform — not a packaged product for any specific niche. The agronomía hypothesis is strong but UNVALIDATED with real humans.

**The fundamental reframe**: ExpoAgro is a **customer discovery and niche validation** trip. The deliverable is not leads or demo commitments — it's KNOWLEDGE. Specifically: *"Which business type has the most acute, urgent operational pain? What does their daily workflow look like? What should Gravitea's first vertical UI be designed around?"*

The previous plan was misallocated because it assumed a sales-ready product. The corrected plan uses the Mom Test methodology: never mention Gravitea, never promise anything, just ask questions and LISTEN.

### Michael Porter (Competitive Strategy)

### Seth Godin (Tribe Building)

**The Provocation**: "Your plan is designed to make YOU feel productive — 4 neat meetings, color-coded. But is it designed to FIND PRODUCT-MARKET FIT?"

**Counter-proposal**: Don't pre-schedule more than 2 meetings. Leave 60% of the day OPEN for serendipity. Carry a one-page flyer with ONE qualifying question. Walk the floor and have 20 conversations of 5 minutes each. The best customer is the one you didn't know existed.

### Nassim Nicholas Taleb (Antifragility)

**The Fragility Audit**: "Your competitive research is beautifully structured — and almost entirely fictional. You've scored companies 5/5 based on desk research and 'Inferred' data. You've never talked to a single one."

**Fragile assumptions in the current plan**:
- "Serquim SA will be receptive" → You don't even have their stand number
- "The person at the stand can make buying decisions" → Expos send junior reps
- "These companies actually need an ERP" → Maybe they solved SENASA with an Excel macro
- "4 meetings = productive day" → If 2 flop (50% probability), you wasted half the day

**Antifragile Redesign**:
- **Optionality**: 2 anchors + 6 options, not 4 fixed meetings
- **Small bets**: 15 conversations of 7-10 min each (tiny downside, massive upside)
- **Barbell**: 80% safe (walk floor talking to every distributor) + 20% aggressive (offer free pilot to desperate prospect ON THE SPOT)

**Key insight**: "The 15-conversation plan gets MORE valuable as things go wrong, because each rejection teaches you something."

## RECOMMENDED ONE-DAY PLAN (Panel-Approved — Discovery Mode)

**See the full revised itinerary in the main research document**: `Gravitea ExpoAgro Competitive Research.md` > "ExpoAgro Visit Plan: Customer Discovery"

### Key Principles

1. **Bring a notebook, not flyers.** No pitch materials. No business cards with "Gravitea ERP."
2. **Cover story**: *"Estoy investigando los problemas administrativos del sector agro."*
3. **80/20 rule**: Listen 80%, ask 20%. Never talk about your solution.
4. **Record everything**: Company name, city, #employees, what they sell, current software, #1 pain, SENASA status.
5. **Review notes at lunch**: Look for PATTERNS — what pain came up 3+ times?

### The 5 Discovery Questions

1. *"¿Qué sistema usan hoy para facturar y manejar el stock?"*
2. *"¿Cuál es la tarea administrativa que más tiempo les consume?"*
3. *"¿Cómo están manejando lo de SENASA 1175 / trazabilidad fitosanitaria?"*
4. *"Si pudieran cambiar UNA cosa de su sistema actual, ¿qué sería?"*
5. *"¿Cuánto pagan hoy por su sistema / contador / solución actual?"*

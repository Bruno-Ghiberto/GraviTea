While classification identifies current states, proactive ERP systems must forecast future states to trigger preventative workflows. The 3DCNN-LSTM hybrid model excels in this predictive domain.10 The 3D-CNN module captures the spatial dependencies across the grain mass, ensuring that the thermal inertia of the grain and the insulative properties of the silo walls are mathematically represented.10 Concurrently, the LSTM module models the dynamic temporal sequences of temperature fluctuations, allowing the system to project the thermal field days or weeks into the future.10

The predictive accuracy of these models is significantly enhanced by incorporating multi-modal data inputs. Relying solely on temperature is insufficient for accurate biological modeling; the inclusion of absolute water potential and relative humidity time-series data drastically reduces prediction error.10 Because humidity and temperature maintain an equilibrium relationship within the interstitial air of the grain pile, algorithms leveraging these combined inputs have achieved a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of just 0.24 °C and a Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) of 0.28 °C in predicting future temperature fields.10 Furthermore, to provide rapid moisture content (MC) estimations without waiting for the sensors to reach perfect equilibrium, advanced edge algorithms utilize curve-fitting techniques on relative humidity (RH) time-series to estimate asymptotic RH, leveraging the Chung-Pfost equilibrium equations to calculate MC to within ±1% accuracy in under 12 minutes.16

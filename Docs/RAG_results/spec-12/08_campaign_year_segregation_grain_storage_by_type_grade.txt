- **Logical segregation**: The AFIP system enforces that stock declarations are per grain per campaign. The ERP mirrors this with a composite key: (plant_id, grain_code, campaign_id).[^18]
- **Physical segregation when possible**: Acopiadores try to empty cells between campaigns, but with limited silo capacity, co-mingling of the same grain from different campaigns is common. - **Carry stock (stock de enlace)**: Grain carried over from a previous campaign into the new one is explicitly tracked. ## How Campaign Affects Acopiador ERP Operations



An acopiador's ERP must track stock along three mandatory axes: **plant → grain → campaign**. Even when physical co-mingling occurs in the same silo cell, the AFIP's systemic registration (RG 3593) requires logical segregation by campaign.[^19][^18]

In practice, this means:

- The same silo cell might contain wheat from campaign 2024/25 and wheat from campaign 2025/26 after the new harvest begins in December.
- The ERP tracks these as separate logical lots. Incoming Cartas de Porte with a CTG (Código de Trazabilidad de Granos) are registered against the corresponding campaign.[^18]
- When the AFIP's online system displays stock for declaration, it shows the last 3 campaigns for operators to fill in quantities per plant.[^24]
- Segregation protocols should be applied where practical, and the INTA recommends strict protocols to minimize contamination between crops and campaigns.[^25][^26]
